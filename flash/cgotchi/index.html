Hey Tattva on Cheeko Gotchi

Cheeko Gotchi v1.2 · one-click install · 5 reels, 5 games, Rock

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop (Safari and phones can't do this).
  2. Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Click Install Hey Tattva, pick the port that appears (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), then Install. Leave "erase" off so the recovery bootloader stays.
  4. Wait until it says done (about a minute), then unplug and plug in again (or press the middle key).

No port in the list, or the port has a long odd name? The Cheeko may be in its recovery mode. Unplug it, plug it back in and try again; some cables only charge, so try another.

On the device: Reels for tattvas 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 (swipe up for the next, tap to pause), Games (Mirror Letters, Grow the Seed, Lamp Tilt, Eclipse, Dream Cards) and Rock (the band from the app with your singer voice). Middle key = back; hold it 5 s to forget Wi-Fi. Side keys = volume. Rock (band + your singing) is built in and plays without Wi-Fi. The reel voice streams live, so connect Wi-Fi for it: join HeyTattva-Setup on your phone and choose your home Wi-Fi.

Other device: Waveshare AMOLED 1.8
