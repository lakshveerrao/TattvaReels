Hey Tattva device

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.8 · one-click install

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop (Safari and phones can't do this).
  2. Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Click Install Hey Tattva, pick the port that appears (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), then Install. Allow it to erase the board.
  4. Wait until it says done (about a minute), then press PWR or unplug and plug in again.
This browser can't install. Please open this page in Chrome or Edge on a laptop. This page must be opened over https.

No port in the list? Hold BOOT, press and release PWR (or unplug and replug while holding BOOT), let go, then click Install again. Some cables only charge; try another.

After installing: pick your language, then on your phone join the Wi-Fi HeyTattva-Setup and choose your home Wi-Fi.
